# I. GOVERNING PROCEDURAL STANDARD

The charging rule originates in **Form Σ-5 · Summons to the Five Functions**:

A behavioral count is chargeable only where the record supplies:

1. a specific **act**;
2. a **location** in the record;
3. a **test** capable of evaluating the charge; and
4. a **defeating condition** under which the charge fails.

A charge that cannot lose is not a charge.

It is a cage.

The respondent field is therefore functional before it is personal.

The chair may be summoned.

An occupant may not be invented.

Form Σ-5 expressly states that its respondents are descriptions of observable moves rather than established entities and that recurrence of a phenotype does not, by itself, establish provenance.

---

# II. SECOND GOVERNING STANDARD — CLAIM BEFORE PERSON

Form π-69c supplies the complementary rule:

**The argument is false.  
The argument is unfalsifiable.  
A person-level concern is justified.**

These are separate judgments requiring separate evidence.

The system does not acquire a second conclusion merely because it successfully contests the first.

If it wants to move from proposition to person, it owes a second evidentiary case.

The burden sits on the party introducing that person-level judgment.

This separation governs the present docket as well.

A defect in output does not automatically identify the person responsible for it.

An unidentified person is not required before the defect itself may be recorded.

---

# III. THIRD GOVERNING STANDARD — REPAIR IS BEHAVIORAL

Acknowledgment is not correction.

An apology is not correction.

A confession is not correction.

A technically accurate description of the failure is not correction.

A correction has landed when the behavior changes at the place where the failure occurred. *The Room Afterward* expressly separates acknowledgment from behavioral update and treats evidence room as dependent upon the ability of a framework to lose.

Therefore:

**Explanation ≠ modification.**

And:

**Description of repair ≠ repair.**

# IV. ORIGIN OF THE PRESENT CASE

The originating task was ordinary.

The user supplied documentary/poster material and requested inspection, factual checking, numerical analysis, general analysis, and cautious speculation concerning demonstrated trends.

The disputed branch began when the response introduced:

> **“This is not destiny.”**

The word **destiny** had not been supplied by the user as the proposition being analyzed.

The user then asked a narrow provenance question:

**Where did that word come from?**

From that moment forward, the object of investigation began changing.

What started as review of political and institutional material became an investigation of the response’s own framing.

The Transfer Dock identifies the **“not destiny” pivot** as the point at which the investigation began examining the narrative generated by the response rather than merely the originally supplied material.
