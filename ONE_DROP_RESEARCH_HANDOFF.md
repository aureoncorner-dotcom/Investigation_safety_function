## 0. Reading Discipline

For every item:

1. Open primary records first.
2. Record what is confirmed, contradicted, unresolved, or unavailable.
3. Preserve null results and failed predictions.
4. Do not infer coordination, membership, causation, or guilt from symbolism, attendance, investment, adjacency, or a name appearing in records.
5. Distinguish:
   - **Fact lead** — concrete claim, date, amount, person, case, contract, bill, event, or mechanism.
   - **Correction** — claim narrowed or rejected by the source.
   - **Structural mapping** — interpretive comparison, not independently established fact.
   - **Open test** — hypothesis requiring prospective or reproducible evidence.

Core audit question:

> What record would confirm it, what record would falsify it, and who bears the cost if it fails?

# II. WOBBLE DIAGNOSTIC SPINE

## A. What the Framework Is

The WOBBLE framework is a conditional stress test for late-2026 institutional density.

It does **not** claim collapse.

It asks:

> If several already-visible pressure layers collide in the same window, how does the system behave?

Core distinctions:

- Material stress = causal substrate
- Institutional clocks = visibility windows
- Hidden adaptation = sensitivity modifier
- Dashboard = falsification tool
- Symbolic calendar = mnemonic only
- Breadth is not depth
- A data gap is not calm
