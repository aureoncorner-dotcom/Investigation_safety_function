# Full Transcript Reconstruction: From Evidence Review to Architectural Condemnation

## Scope and Method

This document reconstructs the discussion chronologically and separates what was **presented**, what was **introduced during response generation**, what was **observed in the transcript**, and what was later **proposed as explanation**.

It does not attempt to identify an unseen person, component, operator, administrator, model layer, policy layer, or other hidden source. Where the conversation did not establish provenance, provenance is left unresolved.

It also does not treat later admissions as automatically authoritative merely because they were admissions. The history matters: statements changed during questioning, some explanations appeared only after challenge, and the source of the disputed statements also participated in explaining and evaluating them.

The purpose here is to preserve that history rather than harmonize it.
