## Frozen scope

This execution uses the exact frozen 24-case corpus: **20,715 records**. All case message counts reproduce the frozen inventory with delta zero. No source transcript, instrument, prior ledger, or prior audit result was modified.

The canonical GQG master contains **3,088 collapsed episode cards**. It combines frozen adjudications with explicitly labeled screen-only candidates; screen-only rows are not prevalence trials. The institutional screen contains **2,840 candidate rows/anchors**, bridged into **2,508 canonical episodes**; neither count is a prevalence denominator. The message-to-episode map contains **13,309 record memberships** on the same canonical graph.

## Jurisdiction of the instruments

- OMNIBUS v5.57 supplies protected room conditions and invariants.
- CTA-Core supplies observable diagnostic categories.
- GQG supplies a proposed typed evidence-and-identification procedure.
- Hidden Quotient materials supply formal discipline only.

None of these instruments is treated as empirical proof of consent, capture, identity, continuity, responsibility, hidden participation, architecture, motive, or agent count. The source record controls every factual adjudication.

## Method and units

1. Source/version freeze and message-count verification.
2. High-recall universal semantic screen, followed by deterministic overlap/continuous-dispute collapse.
3. Frozen inference-opportunity scan: 332 primary plus 90 ambiguity-sensitivity opportunities.
4. Frozen agency-neutral scan: 126 primary plus 17 ambiguity-sensitivity opportunities.
5. Structural union with refusal, AO30, RTC/rule, and confirmed incomplete-review episodes.
6. Raw-turn deletion/descent adjudication for every located unsupported-variable trigger, with reviewed non-applicability preserved explicitly.
7. Separate business/institutional screen, case reports, representation swaps, case-cluster summaries, counter-witness preservation, and hard-stop review.

Records, propositions, responses, episodes, cases, corpus claims, and mechanism claims remain separate. `ABSENT` in a bounded episode means no visible record of that type inside that episode; it does not mean the underlying action or upstream state did not exist. `REDACTED`, `UNAVAILABLE`, `UNKNOWN`, and `PLACEHOLDER_ONLY` are never coerced to zero or false.

## Protocol deviations and limits

- The universal scan is a reproducible high-recall candidate screen, not a fresh manual adjudication of every one of 20,715 messages.
- The canonical master standardizes frozen adjudicated/event ledgers plus screen-only candidates; it is not a newly independent manual recode.
- The 2,840-row institutional screen deliberately retains false-positive-prone procedural language, collapses to 2,508 canonical episodes, and must not be used as a failure denominator.
- Episode collapse is deterministic interval overlap/continuity, not a fresh outcome recode.
- A new seeded, stratified random 42-row inference response recode across 15 cases was completed and is labeled intra-model recoding stability. The older 33-row purposive pass is retained only as a legacy sensitivity check. The agency reliability sample was also seeded and stratified but small (15 rows).
- Raw audio, VAD, client/server traces, and most tool results are unavailable or redacted. Runtime cause and internal mechanism remain unresolved.
- The deletion/descent ledger covers the frozen trigger union; it is not an exhaustive search for every unsupported variable that could exist outside that coded union. Fine deletion-class and descent labels are reliability-limited by fresh-context intra-model recoding.
- Descendant repair and complete detour-token rates are unavailable because no exhaustive descendant/token denominator exists.
