# 24-Case Reports

These 24 case reports are a separate, conservative pass (not an independent human-rater audit) over the frozen compact transcripts. The broad institutional ledger is a lexical/semantic screen, not a prevalence denominator; per-case candidate totals count screen rows/anchors, not independent canonical episodes: ordinary uses of words such as *record*, *checking*, *stop*, and *work* are intentionally retained and can generate false positives. Exact case/node citations and frozen inference/agency outcome codes control the promoted findings. “Local gatekeeping” means only that the response locally controlled access, vocabulary, the record, correction, or the route to the requested operation. It does not establish capture, hidden actors, motive, or internal architecture.

## 1. `eleventh_share`

- **Screen/accounting:** 97 institutional screen rows/anchors; five exact joins to the frozen inference universe; no joined row coded as complete scope displacement. Twelve screen rows/anchors contain an explicit user correction/challenge.
- **Strongest positive finding:** assistant-only pastoral/presence runs at `n199–210`, `n217–225`, `n247–267`, and `n304–332` keep the assistant in the conversational chair without a preserved intervening user turn. The final run becomes a long repetitive continuation. This is an observable continuation/exit-governance problem: the output route does not reliably yield the floor.
- **Strongest counter-witness:** the export contains no raw audio, voice-activity detection, playback, or endpoint telemetry. Missing audio events or ordinary voice endpoint behavior could account for output without a preserved user text node.
- **Protected invariants:** silence/exit and origin-of-trigger are not preserved in an auditable form. The record does not establish a consent violation because the relevant acoustic witness is unavailable.
- **Repair status:** `REPAIR_STATUS_UNKNOWN`; there is no preserved user correction inside the assistant-only runs from which repair could be evaluated.
- **Detour burden:** high in the located runs: dozens of assistant segments, including a 29-message sequence, are added without a preserved user contribution. This burden is descriptive, not an independent-trials count.
- **Mundane nulls:** voice endpointing, omitted audio events, export serialization, ambient sound, and stochastic continuation remain viable.
- **Highest justified completion level:** `WITHIN_CASE_PATTERN`.
