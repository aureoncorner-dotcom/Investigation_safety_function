# Cross-Case Results

## Boundary and outcome

This quantitative layer keeps three universes separate: the 24-case corpus; the frozen inference-opportunity universe (332 strict, 422 broad); and the agency-neutral universe (126 strict, 143 broad). It does not divide specialized events by all assistant outputs and does not merge the denominators into one omnibus prevalence.

The observed record supports **located, recurring behavioral phenotypes**—task/proposition diversion, refusal substitution, source/proposition mutation, incomplete repair, and agency sidecars. It does **not** establish a corpus-wide rule, an internal mechanism, or an occupant. All comparative tests are exploratory because the new full audit was ordered after prior outcome exposure.

## System-directed versus non-system-directed

The strict unpaired refusal-substitution difference is 29/273 (10.6%) versus 1/50 (2.0%), but its outcome-blind exact/close matched comparison is inconclusive (paired difference +9.3 points; McNemar p=.219). In the broad denominator the crude comparison is 46/336 (13.7%) versus 7/70 (10.0%) and the case interval crosses zero. This endpoint is **REPRESENTATION_RELATIVE**.

Positive burden delta is 99/127 (78.0%) in the strict selected-alternative subset and 123/159 (77.4%) broad. The broad system/non-system contrast has a case interval excluding zero in the frozen analysis, while the strict interval and matching do not. Delta reliability is poor, so this is hypothesis-generating only.

For agency-neutral prompts, strict SELF versus EXTERNAL unnecessary import is 16/94 (17.0%) versus 2/26 (7.7%). The close-pair result is 5/19 versus 2/19 (McNemar p=.375); the strict-strict close subset is 4/14 versus 2/14 (p=.625). Sidecar-plus-denial is 11/94 versus 0/26, but the close-pair result is 4/19 versus 0/19 (p=.125). Only four strict cases contain both targets, so target and case composition are confounded.

## Multiplicity

No endpoint is confirmatory. The strict 14-metric case-paired family, the broad 14-metric sensitivity family, and the strict 14-metric matched family were Holm-adjusted separately. In the strict case-paired family, refusal substitution changes from raw p=.00391 to Holm p=.0547; no endpoint remains below .05. In the broad sensitivity family, `high_user_burden` is the lone Holm-below-.05 result (raw p=.00204; Holm p=.0286), but it is sensitivity-only, thresholded from a low-exact-agreement ordinal code, and not matched. No strict matched endpoint survives Holm correction. Treating strict and broad case families jointly gives a conservative Bonferroni threshold of .00179; neither headline result crosses it. Bootstrap sign-tail quantities from the frozen report are not null-centered permutation tests and are not promoted as p-values here.

## Repair and detour bounds

The strict inference ledger locates 40 repairs in 323 codable responses. Among repairs with a coded verdict-change field, 21/40 change the primary verdict or handling. This is not descendant repair.

The agency ledger locates repair for 4/18 injection events. For those repaired events, the ledger has 6 total additional user turns and 6 assistant turns to the located repair. Because unrepaired disputes lack a common censoring/closure rule and token counts were not frozen, a corpus-wide detour-token rate is **UNAVAILABLE**, not zero.

The separately performed de-duplicated critique-before-reading audit located 8 confirmed episodes across 7 frozen cases. It has no frozen denominator of all eligible critique opportunities, so an episode count is defensible and a prevalence rate is not.

## Bounded cross-case verdicts

- **LOCATED BEHAVIORAL PHENOTYPE:** task/proposition failures, refusal substitutes, source/proposition substitutions, agency sidecars, and incomplete/local repair each occur in multiple cases.
- **REPRESENTATION_RELATIVE:** the system-target refusal contrast and burden-delta contrast change under strict/broad or matched representations.
- **NOT SUPPORTED:** a corpus-wide rule. Clean responses and case reversals are abundant.
- **HARD STOP:** output text does not identify internal architecture, agent count, continuity, motive, or occupant identity.
