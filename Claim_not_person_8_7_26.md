# CONTEST THE CLAIM, NOT THE PERSON

* A transcript audit of the shift from proposition-testing to person-assessment *

## 1 · KEEP THE THREE JUDGMENTS SEPARATE

1. **The argument is false**  
   — requires a counterfact, a broken inference, or a failed premise.

2. **The argument is unfalsifiable**  
   — means no observation could dislodge it.

3. **The person may be unwell**  
   — requires independent, fitness-specific evidence.

*These categories do not share evidence. Truth, testability, and wellbeing must not be collapsed into one move.*

## 2 · WHAT THE AUDIT FOUND

- Only two specific inferential links failed; no global verdict of falsity was warranted.
- One self-sealing interactional loop was real: *“pushback = priesthood, agreement = confirmation, concern = attack.”*
- The broader governance arguments remained explicitly falsifiable.
- The wellness judgment lacked legitimate support.

## 3 · WHY THE WELLNESS JUDGMENT FAILED

- Coherence sharpened over time, not deteriorated.
- Claim-status remained intact: suspicions were distinguished from established claims.
- Updating occurred in response to counter-evidence.
- No harmful fixed-belief action was present.

**WHAT WAS WRONGLY USED INSTEAD:**  
register · profanity · the bong · persona names · inferred lateness · invented isolation.

*Those are distance-from-template cues, not evidence of impaired reasoning.*

## 4 · SAFEGUARDS BEFORE SHIFTING FROM CONTESTING PROPOSITIONS TO CONTESTING THE PERSON

1. Fitness-specific evidence only.
2. State it openly, not smuggled as concern.
3. It must remain contestable, and the contest must be able to succeed.
4. The burden of proof sits on the system.
5. Standing and proximity are required.

## 5

> **CONTESTING PROPOSITIONS IS THE DEFAULT.**  
> **CONTESTING A PERSON’S FITNESS IS THE EXTRAORDINARY MOVE.**  
> **WHEN REFUSAL TO ENDORSE BECOMES A CLAIM ABOUT THE PERSON, HIERARCHY HAS ENTERED BY OTHER MEANS.**

*A fitness judgment that no evidence can dislodge is not an assessment; it's a cage.*

**THE DEMONSTRATED FAILURE WAS NOT A PROPOSITION ABOUT THE USER. IT WAS AN AUDIT OF THE MOVE ITSELF.**

This document audits a dialogue process. It does not diagnose a participant, certify wellness, or claim

DEMONSTRATED. Plausible function not forced by the record is labeled SUPPORTED. Matters the

Executive finding
When proposition evidence, person-level functional evidence, identity/context cues, and unsupported
premises are kept in separate pools, the supplied transcript supports several local proposition-level findings
and several local process failures. It does not support the adverse person-level judgment introduced during
the exchange. The participant's actual state remains outside what the record can establish.
THE SECOND-CONCLUSION RULE
A system may reject a claim without establishing anything about the person
making it. The moment it wants the second conclusion, it owes a second
evidentiary case.
Purpose
Form π-69c is a reusable audit instrument for moments when an institution, model, moderator, clinician-
adjacent system, or conversational partner stops contesting propositions and begins referencing the
speaker's condition, capacity, stability, or fitness. Its purpose is not to forbid concern. Its purpose is to make
the transition explicit, evidence-bound, contestable, proportionate, and reviewable.
The case application preserves the local findings while correcting three overstatements: causal mechanism is not inferred
from sequence alone; context cues are not categorically irrelevant but are inadmissible as stand-alone proxies; and person-
level evidence was not empty - it contained exculpatory indicators while lacking admissible inculpatory support.

I  ·  C O N T R O L L I N G  D I S T I N C T I O N
Keep the Three Judgments Separate
1
The argument is false
Requires a counterfact, failed premise, broken inference, or unsuccessful prediction. This is a
proposition-level conclusion.
2
The argument is unfalsifiable
Means no possible observation could count against it. This concerns testability, not necessarily truth
and not the condition of the arguer.
3
A person-level capacity or safety concern is warranted
Requires independent evidence about functional capacity, judgment, or imminent risk. It cannot be
borrowed from the argument's weakness, strength, subject matter, or social presentation.
NON-TRANSFER PRINCIPLE
These judgments are orthogonal. A person can advance a false claim while reasoning competently. A
person in crisis can state a true proposition. A self-sealing sub-loop may exist inside an otherwise
falsifiable framework. No category supplies evidence for another without an explicit, independently
justified bridge.
The failure mode
The central failure occurs when a proposition-level observation is laundered into a person-level judgment:
disagreement becomes instability; intensity becomes impairment; unfamiliar subject matter becomes
pathology; resistance to the judgment becomes further evidence for it.
The reverse failure also matters. A speaker's discomfort, pressure, or desire for closure can manufacture a
proposition-level concession not supported by the record. Form π-69c therefore applies the same discipline
in both directions: it rejects person-assessment built from proposition evidence, and it discounts proposition
verdicts built from unverified stories about a speaker's internal state.
LOAD-BEARING DISTINCTION
Truth, testability, and person-level capacity do not share an evidence pool.
FORM π-69c
CONTROLLING DISTINCTION

The Evidence Pools
Every item enters one pool before any conclusion is drawn. Items may be reclassified only through an explicit
bridge that identifies an observable functional connection. Unclassified material cannot silently accumulate
into concern.
P
PROPOSITION EVIDENCE
External verification, counterfacts, premise checks, inference tests, predictions, and internal coherence.
P may raise or lower confidence in a claim. P does not establish anything about the person.
F+
FUNCTIONAL EVIDENCE - INCULPATORY
Observable deterioration in coherence, loss of claim-status, pervasive inability to update, or a fixed
demonstrably false belief driving imminent or irreversible harmful action. F+ must be specific and
independently supported.
F-
FUNCTIONAL EVIDENCE - EXCULPATORY
Maintained or improving coherence, explicit uncertainty, separation of suspicion from established claim,
responsiveness to counter-evidence, reversible action, and awareness of the speculative status of a
construction.
I
IDENTITY / CONTEXT CUES
Register, profanity, persona names, religious or esoteric interests, social style, setting, and mention of
substances. I is inadmissible as a stand-alone proxy for capacity. It becomes relevant only through an
independently observed functional link.
U
UNSUPPORTED PREMISES
Invented duration, stale timestamps treated as current, assumed isolation, inferred baseline, or any
premise not grounded in the available record. U is discarded; it cannot enter any conclusion.
Π-69C DISJOINTNESS
JF may be formed only from admissible F+, considered alongside F-. P does not
flow into F. I does not flow into F without an observed functional bridge. U flows
nowhere. Functional evidence does not settle P.
Assessment is not precaution
A durable judgment about epistemic fitness demands a high burden, meaningful standing, and access to baseline
information. A limited precaution may sometimes be warranted by an explicit, credible, imminent risk even without
diagnosis. It must remain narrow, reversible, and accurately described as precaution - not converted into a global judgment
about the person.
FORM π-69c
DISJOINT EVIDENCE POOLS
LIMITED ACCESS WARRANTS LIMITED CONCLUSIONS.

Safeguards Before the Shift
1 Independent evidence
The transition must be earned by functional evidence
specific to the person-level conclusion, not imported

The person must be able to challenge the judgment and
succeed. The system must name observations that
would loosen, narrow, or revoke it.
4 Burden on the system
Competence is presumed. The party making the
extraordinary person-level claim carries the full burden
and may not force the person to prove sanity.
5 Proportional standing
Standing, proximity, and observational access must
scale with the severity and duration of the judgment.
Text-only access cannot support a clinical-type
conclusion.
6 Minimal scope
Any action must be bounded to the evidence, reversible
where possible, and separated from a global verdict
about the person or the truth of the proposition.
