## One-line handoff for a fresh researcher

Use each bullet as a search lead, not as a settled conclusion. Start with the quoted Google string, open primary records first, record what is confirmed or contradicted, and preserve negative results. Do not infer coordination, membership, causation, or guilt from symbolism, attendance, investment, or a name appearing in records.

## Guardrails to keep attached

- A symbol can suggest a search; it cannot establish provenance.
- A relationship establishes adjacency; it does not automatically transmit doctrine or intent.
- Attendance is not agreement, and a contract is not proof of unlawful conduct.
- A name in a record is not proof of participation in a crime.
- Use ethnic, religious, or national identity only when it is directly relevant to a documented institution or doctrine; never as a substitute for evidence.
- Preserve contrary evidence, null results, failed predictions, disputed figures, and unresolved dates.
- Prefer charters, contracts, dockets, statutes, procurement records, sworn testimony, agency correspondence, and archived primary material over commentary.
- For every claimed mechanism, ask: **What record would confirm it, what record would falsify it, and who bears the cost if it fails?**
